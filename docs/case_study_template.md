# Varianta case study template

## Business question
Would a redesigned checkout increase purchase conversion without unacceptable customer or business harm?

## Data
Use the application-generated synthetic dataset. Label all results as simulated.

## Design
- Randomization unit: user
- Primary metric: purchases / eligible randomized users
- Significance threshold: record the selected alpha
- Target power and MDE: record the configured design assumptions
- Analysis horizon: record the intended fixed horizon

## Findings
Export the JSON report from the application and paste the computed primary estimate, confidence interval, p-value, sample sizes, validation status, and decision outcome here.

## Business implications
Record the traffic, margin, and order-value assumptions separately from observed synthetic results.

## Limitations
Discuss synthetic data, short-term outcome windows, guardrail testing limits, potential sample-ratio mismatch, multiple comparisons, and lack of valid sequential inference.
